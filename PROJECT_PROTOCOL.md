# Project Protocol

## Title
Real-World Chemoimmunotherapy Use and Survival in Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma

## Basis
This public portfolio project is a methodological reconstruction of a clinical research project in which I participated. It uses synthetic data only.

## Original study framework
- Original data source: National Cancer Database (NCDB)
- Original study period: 2013–2022
- Design: Retrospective observational cohort
- Exposure: First-course chemoimmunotherapy versus chemotherapy alone
- Outcome: Overall survival
- Key analyses: annual treatment distribution, Kaplan–Meier analysis, multivariable Cox regression, treatment-by-year interaction, RMST, and 30-day landmark sensitivity analysis

## Public demonstration
- Synthetic demonstration years shown in figures: 2013–2021
- No restricted NCDB patient-level data are included
- No proprietary team code or unpublished team figures are included
- The public RMST notebook demonstrates the unadjusted RMST concept only; it does not claim to reproduce the original adjusted RMST estimator

## Interpretation principle
Because the original study framework is observational, results are interpreted as adjusted associations rather than automatic causal treatment effects.
